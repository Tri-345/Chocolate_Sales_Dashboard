# Chocolate_Sales_Dashboard

## Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing chocolate sales performance across countries and sales channels, with a dedicated view into salesperson operations over time.

## Dataset
200,000-row chocolate sales dataset, covering orders, revenue, boxes shipped, countries, channels, and salespeople.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Sales Performance
- Total Revenue, Total Boxes Shipped
- Gross Profit Margin
- Total Marketing Spend
- Revenue breakdown (bar chart) and mix (donut chart)
- Detailed performance table
  
  

### 2. Product & Market Analysis
- Interactive slicers by Country and Channel
- Sales by product/category (column chart)
- Country/channel performance (pivot table)
- Scatter analysis of key sales metrics

### 3. Sales Person Operations Analysis
- Performance by salesperson over time (filterable by Order Date)
- Trend line of sales activity
- Detailed operations breakdown (pivot tables)

## Key Insights
- Identifies top-performing countries and sales channels
- Tracks profitability alongside revenue (not just top-line sales)
- Surfaces individual salesperson performance trends over time
- Supports channel and market strategy decisions with drill-down detail

## Tools Used
- Power BI (data modeling, DAX, visualization)
- Excel (data preparation)

## Skills Demonstrated
Data cleaning, DAX measures, interactive dashboard design, KPI reporting, market/channel analysis, stakeholder-ready data storytelling

## Results & Findings
The dashboard showed total revenue of $102.57M across 28M boxes shipped, with Australia as the leading market at $42M, followed by Brazil ($37M), Germany ($11M), and India and Japan tied at $6M each. By channel, Retail was the top-performing channel at 52.44% ($53.79M) of revenue, ahead of Wholesale at 36.54% ($37.48M) and Online at 11.01% ($11.30M), highlighting Retail as the primary channel to prioritize for growth. Net margin after marketing spend stood at a healthy 81.43%, against a total marketing spend of $19.05M, indicating marketing investment converted efficiently into revenue. Year-over-year, marketing spend held steady between 2022 and 2023 (~$9.5M each year) while revenue showed a slight decline, suggesting an opportunity to investigate what shifted in campaign effectiveness or market conditions between the two years. These findings point leadership toward doubling down on the Retail channel and the Australian and Brazilian markets, while reviewing the year-over-year revenue dip to protect marketing ROI going forward.
